# App Roadmap

## Current MVP

- Authentication and role-based navigation
- Player profiles, turf booking, open matches, teams
- Turf owner operations and basic revenue summaries
- Admin oversight, support reports, and compliance screens

## Next Release

- Secure automated account deletion with a Supabase Edge Function
- Production email confirmation and profile creation workflow
- Supabase Storage for turf photos and team logos
- Push notifications and booking reminders
- Razorpay/UPI payment collection and settlement

## Later

- Team invitations and richer member permissions
- Tournament organizer suite, fixtures, and live scoring
- Player rankings, leaderboards, and advanced player stats
- Real-time match chat
- WhatsApp confirmations
- AI match highlights and AI commentary, subject to clear capability and
  privacy disclosures
- Cricket marketplace
- Sponsorship platform
- Advanced owner and admin analytics
- Public web account deletion flow

Roadmap items are directional and should be prioritized after MVP usage,
security review, and store feedback.
